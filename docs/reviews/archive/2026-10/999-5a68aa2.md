## Minor

On CI-sized data the planner takes the exact `blocks_container_idx` path, so the iterative-scan setting is not covered by any test. The PR body admits it doesn't pin the plan; the test name ("the filter applies during the HNSW scan") claims more than it checks.

Note↔code edges between them do exist (27 in my probe). Suggest: "no semantic edge between two of their vaults or two of their repositories".

The full-tier `multilingual-search` run that the PR suggests was not done. It isn't required, since the closed defect's test is fast tier.
