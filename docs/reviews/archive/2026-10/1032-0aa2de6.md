## Minor
- `git-sync.ts:133`: `markMirrorSynced` (`update-ref` plus `gc --auto`, `git-mirror.ts:168-170`) runs after `syncRepositoryFiles` has already written `lastSyncedCommit`, but before the row update that writes `skippedFiles` and `defaultBranch`.
  - If gc fails (disk pressure, for example), a sync that indexed correctly is recorded as a failure.
